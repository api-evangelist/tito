In 2020, after a year of much turmoil (what was that again?), we did something called “Calm December” at the end of the year. The gist was: No formal meetings No deadlines No pressure to work a full day Prioritise self-care At the time, we had cut salaries down to the absolute minimum, and we were doing daily meetings as every day seemed critical to the survival of the company. Things are in a lot better of a place for us right now.
