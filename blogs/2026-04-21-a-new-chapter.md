Down through the years, we’ve poked a bit of fun at companies who have written things like “opening our Irish office”, or “we’ve raised money”, or “we’re continuing our amazing journey”, when they actually meant “avoiding tax”, or “engaging in a hail mary plan to save the company”, or “we’ve been hired by a bigger company who doesn’t care about our product”. In 2025, Team Tito Limited, the company, turned 10, which is a pretty good milestone to reach. We never triple tripled or double doubled.
